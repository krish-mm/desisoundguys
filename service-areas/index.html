Where we play

Desi Sound Guys is a South Asian wedding DJ and production crew out of Birmingham, Alabama. These are cities we travel to. Some already have nights on the recap blog.

Book DSG Event recaps

Some of these cities already have nights on the recap blog. Some are dates we travel for. Either way, we talk about your venue, your events and the production.

The job is the same wherever the weekend is: DJ, audio, lights, effects, dhol, mobile baraat sound, emcee, plus photobooths and media when you want them. The set blends Desi, English and Spanish, including Bollywood, Bhangra and Punjabi.

We serve events all across the USA. Pricing comes after a free consultation. Start with your city, or go straight to the booking form.
