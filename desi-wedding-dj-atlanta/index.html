Asked and answered

Have you DJed in Atlanta?

Yes. We’ve played a Bollywood DJ Night with Dhoom Emory at Emory University, a sangeet in Atlanta, and a high school graduation party in Atlanta.

Do you do sangeet, baraat and garba?

Sangeet and baraat, yes — we specialize in multi-day South Asian weddings. We’ve already played a sangeet in Atlanta, and a mundan with garba at Shree Shakti Mandir in Georgia.

Do you play Bollywood and Punjabi?

Yes. Desi, English, Spanish. Bollywood, Bhangra, Punjabi and more. The Emory night was a Bollywood DJ Night.

Do you only play campus events?

No. The Emory night is one recap. We’ve also been in Georgia banquet halls, including Al-Noor Banquet Hall for mehndi, pithi and a wedding first dance.

Are you based in Atlanta?

No. We’re based in Birmingham, Alabama, and we travel to Atlanta.

How do we book?

Send the form or call. You hear back within 24 hours. We recommend booking at least 6 months out.
