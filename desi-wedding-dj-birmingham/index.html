Asked and answered

Do you DJ desi weddings in Birmingham, AL?

Yes. Birmingham is home base. We’ve played wedding receptions, sangeets, baraats, engagements and festival nights here, including a fusion reception at Regions Field and productions at The Club and the Hyatt Regency.

Do you do sangeet, baraat and garba?

Sangeet and baraat, yes. We specialize in multi-day South Asian weddings, from mehndi and sangeet through baraat, ceremony and reception. We’ve also played garba and dandiya nights in Birmingham, including a Bollywood Garba Night at Silver Coin Indian Grill, Dandiya Utsav at UAB Greens, and Navratri garba at City Walk.

Do you play Bollywood and Punjabi?

Yes. Desi, English, Spanish. Bollywood, Bhangra, Punjabi and more. We blend genres and languages so every generation hits the floor.

Can you do the whole wedding weekend?

Yes. We specialize in multi-day South Asian weddings. Packages depend on the events, so tell us every one on the form: mehndi, sangeet, baraat, ceremony, reception.

Do you only play Birmingham?

No. We’re based in Birmingham and serve events all across the USA. We’ve already played nights in cities like Nashville, Memphis, Atlanta, Huntsville and New Orleans — recaps are on the event blog.

How much does a desi wedding DJ in Birmingham cost?

Every night is custom, so pricing comes after a free consultation. Your quote depends on how many events, how many hours and what production you add.

How far ahead should we book?

We recommend booking at least 6 months before your event. A 35% deposit locks your date.
