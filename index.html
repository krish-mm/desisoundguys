Lighting show

What it is
Uplights set on the floor that wash walls, drapes and columns in your colors, plus moving heads that pan, tilt and change color, throwing beams and patterns programmed to hit with the music.
Why choose it
Uplights are the easiest way to change how the whole room looks, from dinner through the last song. Moving heads make the dance floor feel like a concert and move with the music.
When to skip it
If your venue already has built-in lighting you love, or it is an outdoor daytime event where the color won't show. Skip the moving heads for a quiet ceremony or a sit-down dinner where you want the room calm and still.

Cold sparks

What it is
Machines that shoot up fountains of sparks for a fireworks look without real fireworks.
Why choose it
They frame the big moments: grand entrances, first dances and the last song.
When to skip it
Outdoors, since they depend on wind and weather, and check with your venue on ceiling height and whether they're allowed.

Spark guns

What it is
A handheld spark effect, aimed and fired by hand right on the drop.
Why choose it
It puts a burst of sparkle right where the energy is, in the middle of the crowd.
When to skip it
Outdoors, where wind and weather call the shots, or at a low-key event, and check with your venue first.
[Add: photo]

CO2 guns

What it is
Handheld blasters that shoot a cold white plume of CO2 over the crowd.
Why choose it
Fired on the drop, they give the dance floor a jolt of hype.
When to skip it
If you want an elegant, classy vibe.

LED dance floor

What it is
A dance floor built from LED panels that light up in colors and patterns.
Why choose it
The floor itself becomes part of the show and pulls people onto it.
When to skip it
If you love your venue's floor as it is or want a classic, understated look.

LED video wall

What it is
A big screen made of LED panels for visuals, names, monograms, photos or live video.
Why choose it
It gives the stage a backdrop that changes with every part of the night.
When to skip it
For a small, intimate room where a big screen would take over the space.

Vinyl wraps

What it is
Custom printed vinyl laid over the dance floor with your names, monogram or design.
Why choose it
It makes the floor match your event and shows up great in photos from above.
When to skip it
If you are already on an LED dance floor or the floor won't be seen much.
[Add: photo]

Dance on cloud

What it is
Thick fog that stays low and hugs the floor.
Why choose it
It creates the dancing-on-the-clouds look for a first dance.
When to skip it
For a graduation or a high-energy party where nobody is doing a slow dance.

How much does it cost?

Every night is custom, so pricing comes after a free consultation. Your quote depends on how many events, how many hours and what production you add. Send the form below to book yours.

Do you travel?

Yes. We’re based in Birmingham, AL and serve events all across the USA. The travel fee depends on the distance and the gear, and your free consultation sets it.

How far ahead should we book?

We recommend booking at least 6 months before your event. A 35% deposit locks your date.

What music do you play?

Desi, English, Spanish. Bollywood, Bhangra, Punjabi and more. We blend genres and languages so every generation hits the floor.

Can we send must-play and do-not-play lists?

Yes. Your must-plays are locked in and your do-not-plays stay off. We read the room for everything in between.

Are you insured?

Yes. We’re insured for $1M.

What if the power or the DJ falls through?

Power: we bring our own power stations, and our lights run on batteries. DJ: we’ve got a team of 5+ DJs, so there’s always backup.

Yes. We specialize in multi-day South Asian weddings, so we know our way around a sangeet-to-reception weekend. Packages depend on the events, so tell us every one on the form: mehndi, sangeet, baraat, ceremony, reception.
