Uplights

LED lights set on the floor that wash walls, drapes and columns in your colors. Paired with LED tubes, they set the look of the room from dinner through the last song.

Moving lights

Intelligent fixtures that pan, tilt and change color, throwing beams, spots and gobo patterns across the room. Cued over DMX so they move with the music.

Lasers

Thin, sharp beams of colored light that sweep and fan out over the crowd. They turn a dance floor into a club.

Cold sparks

Machines that shoot up fountains of sparks, a fireworks look without real fireworks. Made for entrances, first dances and big moments.

Spark guns

A handheld spark effect, aimed and fired by hand. A burst of sparkle right where the energy is, on the drop.

CO2 guns

Handheld blasters that shoot a cold white plume of CO2 over the crowd. Fired by hand on the drop to lift the room.

CO2 jets

Cannons that blast tall columns of white CO2 fog straight up, timed to the music for drops, entrances and big moments.

LED dance floor

A dance floor built from LED panels that light up in colors and patterns, so the floor itself is part of the show.

LED video wall

A big screen made of LED panels for visuals, names, monograms, photos or live video behind the DJ or stage.

Vinyl wraps

Custom printed vinyl laid over the dance floor with your names, monogram or design, so the floor matches the event.

Low-lying fog

Thick fog that stays low and hugs the floor for a dancing-on-the-clouds look.

Indoor snow

Snow-like flakes that drift down over the room for a winter-wonderland moment, indoors.

Also: custom monograms and name washes, and a custom DJ booth.
