Uplights

What it is
LED lights set on the floor that wash walls, drapes and columns in your colors.
Why choose it
It is the easiest way to change how the whole room looks, from dinner through the last song.
When to skip it
If your venue already has built-in lighting you love, or it is an outdoor daytime event where the color won't show.

Moving lights

What it is
Smart fixtures that pan, tilt and change color, throwing beams and patterns programmed to hit with the music.
Why choose it
They make the dance floor feel like a concert and move with the music.
When to skip it
For a quiet ceremony or a sit-down dinner where you want the room calm and still.

Lasers

What it is
Thin, sharp beams of colored light that sweep and fan out over the crowd.
Why choose it
Nothing turns a dance floor into a club faster.
When to skip it
If the night is more dinner than dance floor, and check with your venue first since some don't allow them.

Cold sparks

What it is
Machines that shoot up fountains of sparks for a fireworks look without real fireworks.
Why choose it
They frame the big moments: grand entrances, first dances and the last song.
When to skip it
Outdoors, since they depend on wind and weather, and check with your venue on ceiling height and whether they're allowed.

Spark guns

What it is
A handheld spark effect, aimed and fired by hand right on the drop.
Why choose it
It puts a burst of sparkle right where the energy is, in the middle of the crowd.
When to skip it
Outdoors, where wind and weather call the shots, or at a low-key event, and check with your venue first.
[Add: photo]

CO2 guns

What it is
Handheld blasters that shoot a cold white plume of CO2 over the crowd.
Why choose it
Fired on the drop, they give the dance floor a jolt of hype.
When to skip it
If you want an elegant, classy vibe.

CO2 jets

What it is
Cannons that blast tall columns of white CO2 fog straight up, timed to the music.
Why choose it
They make the loud moments louder: drops, entrances and takeovers.
When to skip it
For a soft, romantic or formal evening, or a small room where the blast would feel like too much.

LED dance floor

What it is
A dance floor built from LED panels that light up in colors and patterns.
Why choose it
The floor itself becomes part of the show and pulls people onto it.
When to skip it
If you love your venue's floor as it is or want a classic, understated look.

LED video wall

What it is
A big screen made of LED panels for visuals, names, monograms, photos or live video.
Why choose it
It gives the stage a backdrop that changes with every part of the night.
When to skip it
For a small, intimate room where a big screen would take over the space.
[Add: photo]

Vinyl wraps

What it is
Custom printed vinyl laid over the dance floor with your names, monogram or design.
Why choose it
It makes the floor match your event and shows up great in photos from above.
When to skip it
If you are already on an LED dance floor or the floor won't be seen much.
[Add: photo]

Low-lying fog

What it is
Thick fog that stays low and hugs the floor.
Why choose it
It creates the dancing-on-the-clouds look for a first dance.
When to skip it
For a graduation or a high-energy party where nobody is doing a slow dance.

Indoor snow

What it is
Snow-like flakes that drift down over the room.
Why choose it
It gives you a winter-wonderland moment indoors, any time of year.
When to skip it
If you are going for a summer garden vibe.
