Desi wedding in Nashville? We’ve been here before, and we brought the whole show.

Desi Sound Guys is based in Birmingham, Alabama, and Nashville is a city we keep coming back to. We’ve shut down streets for a baraat, run lighting and production at the Parthenon, and held wedding weekends that stretched from garba and sangeet to the last song of the reception.

Asked and answered

Do you DJ desi weddings in Nashville?

Yes. We’ve already played wedding weekends in Nashville, including Priyanka and Deep’s garba, sangeet, baraat and Parthenon reception, plus other Nashville receptions and sangeets on the recap blog.

Have you actually played the Parthenon?

Yes. We’ve run lighting and production there, and we’ve been on Parthenon wedding receptions — including Priyanka and Deep’s reception and A Night with Athena.

Do you do baraats in Nashville?

Yes. Ishan’s baraat shut down the streets of Nashville, and Deep’s baraat was part of a Nashville wedding weekend. Mobile baraat sound and dhol are part of what we bring.

Do you do sangeet and garba in Nashville?

Yes. Tony and Ashna’s Nashville sangeet went from Bollywood to ABBA and Britney. Priyanka and Deep’s weekend included garba and sangeet in Nashville.

Are you based in Nashville?

No. We’re based in Birmingham, Alabama, and we travel to Nashville. You still get the full DJ and production crew — we’re just not a Nashville-only shop.

Do you play Bollywood and Punjabi?

Yes. Desi, English, Spanish. Bollywood, Bhangra, Punjabi and more. We blend genres and languages so every generation hits the floor.

Can you work Gaylord Opryland or other Nashville venues?

We’ve already DJed a first birthday at Gaylord Opryland. For any venue, we’ll confirm production rules, including effects like cold sparks, during planning.

How do we book a Nashville date?

Send the booking form or call. You hear back within 24 hours. We recommend booking at least 6 months out; a 35% deposit locks the date.
